and adjudicated on the unchanged 190-source F002 freeze. Every report and seal was
rehash-checked before this public curation. No confirmed load-bearing error or
open potentially load-bearing allegation remained after the third round.

certificate. AI checks can share correlated failure modes. Finite controls and
individual probe recompilations are not represented as additional whole-project

## Round 2: residue scales, inverse presentations and singular geometry

The reviews attacked the use of predecessor-field tolerances, coalescing roots,
boundedness, denominator normalization, nonreduced finite presentations and
root-enumeration dependence. The descent uses an algebraic annihilator and all
predecessor tolerances, including older infinitesimal scales; it does not assume
an unrestricted standard part for every bounded non-Archimedean element or
rational density. Singular presentation controls strip zero factors before
inversion, rather than inverting a singular ambient multiplication matrix.

Independent physical fixtures showed why nonsingular inverse blocks and a
nonreal loop do not by themselves exclude `D = 0`, and why trace/purity checks
without an already-proved kernel are insufficient. The actual formal branches
supply the missing guards. Projective/fiber controls demonstrated that degree six
and absence of infinity alone do not imply unique affine recovery; the stronger
formal guard is essential. Repeated sextic roots can still satisfy that guard.

The normalization error in original Eq. (35) was independently reproduced:
its ordinary resultant quotient is 100 times the displayed primitive polynomial.
The nonzero factor does not alter root/sign/coverage conclusions. The erratum
remains mandatory.
